Privacy Policy — RaveWatchLagos

Last updated: 22 October 2026

RaveWatchLagos ("the app") is an event-discovery app for raves and DJ-led nightlife in Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

What the app does NOT collect

  • No user accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No location data (the app never asks for location permission).
  • No contacts, photos, or any other personal files.
  • No payment information (ticket purchases happen on the organiser's or ticketing provider's own website, outside the app).

What the app does handle

Anonymous attending counts. On an event page you can tap "Yes" or "No" to say whether you're attending. That tap sends only the event's ID and a +1/−1 change to our counter service (Supabase), which keeps a single running total per event. No identifier of any kind — no name, account, device ID or IP-based profile — is attached to or stored with your tap. The totals are anonymous aggregates shown to all users as an estimate of attendance.

On-device preferences. The app stores your followed events, theme choice (night/light) and similar settings in local storage on your phone only. Counts of your own in-app actions (for example, which events you opened or shared) are also kept on your phone only, for a usage summary shown on the device. None of this leaves your phone.

Event feed. The app downloads a public list of events (names, dates, venues, prices, ticket links) from a public file host (GitHub). Downloading that list does not send any personal information about you.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone — we do not hold any. The anonymous attending totals are public aggregates visible to every user of the app.

Children

The app lists nightlife events, many of which are 18+. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
